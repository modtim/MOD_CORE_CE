Custom Registry

Push Your Custom Images

A custom Docker registry is not part of this edition.

  • Use Docker Hub, your own registry, or any OCI-compliant registry
  • Full control over your custom images
  • Automatic fallback to platform images
Open settings

Example: docker.io, registry.example.com, gcr.io

How it works:

  1. System tries to pull images from your custom registry first
  2. If image not found, falls back to platform registry
  3. Platform images are always available
